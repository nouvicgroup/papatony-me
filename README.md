# PapaTony.me

The official professional profile of **Apostle Dr. Anthony Nkumbe**, known as **Papa Tony** — a Cameroon-based entrepreneur, certified real estate consultant, investment facilitator, institution builder and ministry founder.

This repository holds the source of that published site. It is a single, purpose-built profile for one person, not a template or a starter to be cloned.

---

## Who Papa Tony is

Anthony Nkumbe trained as an agronomist at FASA, University of Dschang — a formation that began with land, production and the practical realities of local development. His work widened from there into property, enterprise, cooperatives and investment facilitation, while his ministry life gave the moral authority that runs underneath all of it.

He is best understood as a **connector**: someone who helps founders, property owners, investors and institutions qualify an opportunity in Cameroon, meet the right local counterparts, and reach an informed next step.

Someone hears Papa Tony's name before a meeting, an invitation, a partnership or an introduction, and looks him up. The site exists to give that person an accurate, dignified and useful picture — credible to investors, municipalities and institutions, and warm enough for the ministry community.

## Editorial position: proof before claims

The site leads with **roles held, not results claimed**. That is a deliberate choice, and it shapes the whole homepage.

Standing here comes from positions Papa Tony currently holds. Outcomes belong to the parties involved and are not advertised. The site states plainly what is still being documented — named engagements with dates and geography, measurable results confirmed by the parties involved, and attributed references published with permission — and leaves structured space for each to be added once verified.

Nothing on the site invents a case study, a project total, an award, a client name, a testimonial or a metric. Papa Tony is presented as a real estate consultant and investment facilitator, **not** as a financial adviser or wealth manager, and no investment outcome is promised anywhere.

The inquiry form is honest in the same way: until an official destination channel is published, it validates the message, says clearly that nothing has been sent, and lets the visitor copy what they wrote. It never fakes a successful submission.

## Experience

**Desktop** is an editorial executive profile — open bands, hairline rules and generous whitespace rather than stacks of cards.

**Mobile is a distinct experience, not a narrower desktop.** It opens with a three-slide branded introduction on first visit, then a layered hero where the portrait leads and the copy panel rises over its lower half. Engagement routes are a swipeable pager, the inquiry opens as a bottom sheet, and a fixed tab bar carries the main sections. The introduction respects reduced-motion preferences and never replays.

Identity is a **PapaTony** logotype whose medial *T* carries the accent colour, paired with a mark of that same *T* standing on a plinth.

## Photography and likeness

Portraits on the site are **AI-composited images of a real person in constructed settings**, produced under a strict likeness rule: Papa Tony's facial identity, age, build and natural presence are preserved, never slimmed, enlarged or beautified. Several generated variants were rejected for exactly that reason.

They are presented as **portraiture only**. No image is captioned or implied to document a specific event, project, client, office or location. Construction imagery is editorial.

One photograph on the site is genuine documentary material rather than a composite, and the ministry and institutional marks are real.

No portrait of Rev. Carine Nkumbe has been approved, so the family and legacy section is intentionally text-only and implies no missing image.

## Technical notes

Next.js on Vite via [vinext](https://github.com/cloudflare/vinext), React, TypeScript and plain global CSS, deployed as a Cloudflare Worker. Self-hosted Geist. Bilingual routing with canonicals, hreflang, sitemap, robots and Person/WebSite/ProfilePage structured data.

Quality gates: ESLint, a production build, server-render tests covering every route in both languages, and a Playwright suite covering navigation, the mobile introduction, the tab bar, the inquiry sheet, keyboard focus and the honest form state.
